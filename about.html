About Me

Creative Technologist

I'm a full stack developer and creative technologist based in Bristol and London. I hold a BA in Music and an MSc in Computer Science from the University of Bristol.

My unique background allows me to bridge the gap between artistic vision and technical execution. I build interactive web applications, compose experimental electronic music, and design instruments that explore the intersection of software and hardware.

Full Stack Development

I am aiming to specialize in building end-to-end applications with React, TypeScript, and .NET. My focus is on creating clean, maintainable code and thoughtful user experiences, particularly tools designed for creative workflows and interactive media.

Music & Sound Design

I compose experimental electronic music exploring granular synthesis, field recording, and sonic manipulation. I also design audio experiences for interactive media.

Experimental Instruments

I design hybrid hardware/software instruments using sustainable materials, embedded electronics, and innovative interfaces.
