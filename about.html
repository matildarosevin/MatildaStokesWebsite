About Matilda Stokes

Creative Technologist • Full Stack Developer • Musician

Who I Am

I'm a creative technologist and full stack developer based in Bristol, with a unique background spanning music, art, and computer science. I'm passionate about building interactive experiences that challenge conventions and create new possibilities for creative expression.

My journey started as a musician—I studied Music at the University of Bristol—but I discovered that my true passion lies at the intersection of art and technology. After completing my MSc in Computer Science, I've been building web applications, experimenting with interactive tools, and exploring how code can enable new forms of artistic practice.

I believe the best creative technologists are those who understand both sides: the artistic vision and the technical execution. I bring curiosity, attention to detail, and a collaborative spirit to every project I work on.

My Practice

Full Stack Development
I build web applications using React, TypeScript, and .NET. I'm focused on creating clean, maintainable code and thoughtful user experiences. Recent projects include interactive tools for creative workflows, demonstrating how full stack development can serve artistic practice.

Electronic Music & Sound Design
I compose and produce experimental electronic music, explore granular synthesis, and design audio experiences for interactive media. My work often investigates how technology can create new sonic possibilities.

Experimental Instrument Design
I design and build hybrid software/hardware instruments that explore the intersection of art and engineering. My Ceramic Granular Synthesiser is an example of this practice—combining sustainable materials, embedded electronics, and innovative interfaces.
