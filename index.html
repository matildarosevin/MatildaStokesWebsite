About

I'm a creative technologist bridging art, music, and software engineering. I hold a BA in Music from the University of Bristol and an MSc in Computer Science, combining artistic vision with technical rigor.

My practice spans electronic music production, full stack web development, and experimental instrument design. I'm passionate about creating interactive experiences that challenge how we think about creativity and technology.

Currently focused on React, TypeScript, and .NET development. I'm fascinated by how code can enable new forms of artistic expression—whether through interactive web tools, algorithmic composition, or custom hardware.

I'm preparing for my dream role as a Full Stack Developer at Aardman Studios, where I hope to bring my unique blend of creative and technical skills to their groundbreaking animation and interactive projects.
